Privacy

Golfers Practice Hub, run by Karina Sánchez Golf LLC, Charlotte, NC. Last updated October 30, 2026.

The short version

  • We collect only what the app needs to help you practice and track your golf.
  • We never sell your information or use it for ads.
  • In the practice version, your stats are private unless you choose to share them with Karina.
  • You can delete your account and everything in it at any time.

What we collect

  • Your email and first name, to send your sign-in code and show your name in the app.
  • The golf information you enter: rounds and stats, practice sessions, goals, club distances, shot patterns, game results and your notes.
  • Karina's students only: lesson notes, drills, practice plans, fitness results, and the swing videos, pictures and PDFs you or Karina upload.
  • When you last opened the app, so Karina can see who's active.

We don't collect payment details, your location, or your contacts. The app has no ads and no tracking.

Who can see it

  • Practice version (newsletter sign-ups): only you, unless you turn on "Let Karina see my stats" or ask to work with Karina. Without that, Karina only sees totals, like how many golfers signed up and how many practiced this week. She can't see your name or your data.
  • Karina's students: you and Karina. If a parent signs in for a junior player, the parent sees that player's space too.
  • No other user of the app can see your information.

Services that help run the app

These companies store or process data only so the app can work:

  • Supabase: the database and sign-in.
  • Cloudflare: hosts the app.
  • Resend: sends sign-in codes and app emails.
  • Mux: stores and plays swing videos (Karina's students only).
  • Anthropic: powers the optional AI features, like round reviews and tailored practice (Karina's students only). Your stats are sent to write the suggestion and aren't used to sell anything to you.

Emails

You get a sign-in code when you ask for one. Karina's students also get an email when Karina sends a note, a video or lesson notes. Being in the app doesn't add you to any mailing list.

Your device

The app keeps you signed in and saves unfinished practice on your phone so nothing is lost if you close it. That information stays on your device.

Age

The practice version is for golfers 18 and older. Junior players use the app only as Karina's students, with a parent's email.

Keeping or deleting your data

  • Practice version: open the menu (your initials, top right) and tap Delete my account and all my data. It's removed right away.
  • Karina's students: email Karina and she'll delete your space. If you stop lessons, your space is archived: sign-in stops working, and your history is kept unless you ask for it to be deleted.

Questions

Email karina@karinagolfcoaching.com. If this page changes, the date at the top changes too.
